# TOC

The workspace's index, in [zTOC format](agent-data/notes.md#ztoc-format).

## Ladder

- 0 (done) docs: CONTRIBUTING.md names the project's tasks

## The TOC

- [TODO.md](TODO.md): the todo list and the cycle-record, in the [Todo
  format](agent-data/notes.md#todo-format).

- [todo-backlog.md](notes/todo-backlog.md): the long-tail Todos.
- [bugs.md](notes/bugs.md): the bugs.
